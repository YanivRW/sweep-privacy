Privacy Policy — Sweep

Effective date: November 10, 2026  •  App: Sweep (Android com.yaniv.sweep)
Contact: yanivrw2@gmail.com

Sweep ("the app", "we") helps you clean up your phone's photos, videos and audio files by swiping to keep or delete them. This policy explains what the app accesses and how your information is handled.

Summary

  • Sweep reads the photos, videos and audio files on your device only to show (and play) them to you inside the app so you can review and delete them.
  • Your photos, videos and audio files never leave your device. We do not upload, copy, store, or transmit them to any server. We have no server and no account system.
  • Erase by face groups your photos by the people in them entirely on your device. The face data it creates never leaves your device, and turning the feature off in Settings deletes all of it.
  • The app shows ads via Google AdMob, which collects limited device data to serve those ads (see "Advertising" below).

Information the app accesses

  • Photos and videos (and their metadata such as size, date, album). Accessed via Android media permissions solely to display your library inside the app and to delete items you choose. This content is processed on your device and is never sent anywhere by us.
  • Audio files (and their metadata such as size, date, duration, folder) — for example call recordings, voice recorder files and audio saved by messaging apps. Accessed via Android's audio permission, which Sweep asks for only when you open the Audio section (or turn on Audio in Settings), solely to list them, play them back inside the app and delete the ones you choose. Music, ringtones and notification sounds are skipped. Audio is processed on your device and is never sent anywhere by us.
  • Faces in your photos (Erase by face). When Erase by face is on (it is on by default and can be turned off in Settings), Sweep looks for faces in your photos so it can group photos of the same person and show you, for example, "128 photos" of one person. For each face it finds, Sweep computes a face template (a list of numbers that describes the face) and keeps it, together with the photo's id and the position of the face, plus one small cropped picture per person for the list of people, in Sweep's private storage on your device. This runs in the background — also when Sweep is closed — and checks new photos about once a week. Face templates may be considered biometric data in some places. They are created and stored on your device only: they are never uploaded, shared or sold, never sent to us or anyone else, and Sweep does not know or ask who anyone is (there are no names). Only photos are checked — never videos, audio files or other files. Turning Erase by face off in Settings deletes all face data immediately, and uninstalling Sweep deletes it too.
  • App settings and review choices (e.g. which items you marked "keep", your selected batch size and keep-duration). Stored locally on your device only.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect, upload, or share your photos, videos, audio files, face data, or their contents.
  • We do not require an account, name, email, or sign-in.
  • We have no analytics or tracking SDK of our own.

Deleting items

When you delete an item, Sweep uses Android's system delete request: the OS shows a confirmation dialog, and only after you approve is the item removed. Deletion is permanent — the file is erased from your device and the space is freed immediately. Sweep never deletes anything without your confirmation.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sweep displays ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, device information, coarse/approximate location, and ad interaction data. This is handled by Google, not by us. Google's use of this data is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads).

Your choices and rights (GDPR / CCPA)

  • You can revoke Sweep's photo/video or audio permission at any time in your device Settings → Apps → Sweep → Permissions.
  • You can turn off Erase by face at any time in Sweep → Settings; this stops face scanning and deletes all face data from your device.
  • For users in regions requiring consent (e.g. EU/EEA, UK), a consent prompt for personalized ads may be shown before ads are personalized.
  • To request information about data handled through AdMob, contact us at the email above; data processed by Google is subject to Google's policies.

Children

Sweep is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
